Support

Gridiron Duel, by playbyear.

Gridiron Duel is a turn-based football game where you're the coordinator. Pick your personnel, call a play on offense or defense, and watch the snap play out. Play the computer, a friend on one device, or a friend online. It works fully with VoiceOver and other screen readers.

Get help or send feedback

Use the contact form for bug reports, questions and ideas, and say that it's about Gridiron Duel. You don't need an account. Add your email address if you'd like a reply. For a bug, it helps to say what you were doing and whether you use VoiceOver.

Common questions

How do I play?

The full rules are in the game, under How to play at the top of every screen. In short: each snap, both coaches pick personnel and call a play without seeing the other's call. Then the play runs and the result is announced. Some calls beat others, so mix up what you call.

How do I play a friend online?

Tap Create a game, set up your team, and send your friend the invite or the four-letter game code. Your friend types the code and taps Join, or opens the invite link. Each of you plays on your own device. A friend on the website, on itch.io and in the iPhone app can all play each other.

I closed the game in the middle of an online game.

Open it again. The iPhone app and the website rejoin the game on their own. If they don't, type the game code and tap Join, and you're back in your seat.

It says an update is needed.

Your friend's copy of the game is newer than yours, and both coaches need the same version. In the iPhone app, update Gridiron Duel from the App Store. On the website, reload the page.

Does it need the internet?

Games against the computer and on one device work offline. Only playing a friend online needs a connection.

How hard is the computer?

Pick Rookie, Pro or All-Pro. Pro and All-Pro chart your play calls and start calling the counter, so don't get predictable.

Does it have ads, purchases or tracking?

No ads, nothing to buy, no accounts and no tracking. See the privacy policy.
